3. Mathematical Proof (P2 Decode)

MMON_SLAVE (Session 322/45335) was blocked on "library cache: mutex X" with:
    P1 = 1950821498
    P2 = 13237089206272
    P3 = 124

In Oracle 11.2, the holder SID is encoded in P2's upper 32 bits:
    TRUNC(13237089206272 / POWER(2,32)) = 3082

This mathematically confirms SID 3082 was the mutex holder. We have 62 consecutive ASH samples (08:47:12 through 09:23:03) showing this same blocking relationship — every single sample points to Session 3082/44313 from drvl1078.

Session 322 (MMON_SLAVE), while waiting for Session 3082, became the secondary blocker — accumulating 102,342 wait events from 2,878 unique sessions. This is the cascading chain that brought the database down.

- References Change Request CR2028 and Defects DEFECT-20418, DEFECT-20608
