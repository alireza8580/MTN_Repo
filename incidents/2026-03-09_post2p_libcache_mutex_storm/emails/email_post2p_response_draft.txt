This 100,000x increase in library cache mutex wait time, with no change in workload, means something caused mass cursor invalidation on March 9th. The internal library cache latch statistics confirm this:

                         March 7         March 9 (incident)
kgllkdl1 (lock dealloc)  68,880 gets     1,487,021,925 gets (21,600x increase)
kglhdgn1 (handle get)    78,331 gets     936,986,368 gets (12,000x increase)

These mutexes are involved in cursor deallocation and reallocation. This is a definitive indicator of mass cursor invalidation — all cursors in the library cache were invalidated, forcing every session to re-parse every SQL statement simultaneously, which serialized the entire library cache.
