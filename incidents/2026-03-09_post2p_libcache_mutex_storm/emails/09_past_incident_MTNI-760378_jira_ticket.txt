SM Conclusion:

 

Cause:

There was a huge load on Ability database caused ora**e background processes to fail.

Impact:

• DPOS registration success rate is dropped to Zero

• CLM portal success rate is dropped to around 20%

• Subscribers are unable to connect to some UMS IVR pilots

• MFS erefill success rate is dropped to 97%

• NGPG success rate is dropped to 93%

• Postpaid subscribers are unable to login to MyIrancell application

• Buyable Offer activation success rate from USSD, MyIrancell (App and Web) and eShop channels is dropped to 2%

• AAT Lending on MyIrancell channel is impacted

• iChat portal has fluctuation

Duration time: 15 May 10:45 to 13:15.

Support killed blocking sessions and then bounced the CBS database via CAT1 Emergency Change MTNI-760463.
